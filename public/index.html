Core rules

One Action Point. Four choices. Last player standing.

MoveSpend 1 AP to move one square up, down, left or right.
AttackSpend 1 AP to hit a player within your range. Three hits eliminate a player.
TransferSpend 1 AP to give 1 AP to another player within your range.
Extend rangeSpend 1 AP to permanently increase your range by one square.

Everyone starts with 1 AP, 3 lives and range 2. Range includes horizontal, vertical and diagonal squares (square / Chebyshev distance). Movement is orthogonal only. Players cannot occupy the same square. Living players receive 1 AP every day at 20:00 Japan time. The database applies this even when everyone is offline.
