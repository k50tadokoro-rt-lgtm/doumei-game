A persistent match for up to 9 players · 10, 12 or 15 squares wide
Browser multiplayer · 9 seats
Set up a match

Create a new game

Choose all nine countries / player names. Your friends can claim their seats with the room code and their exact names. Seats nobody claims never appear on the map. Once you press Start, no one else can join.

Join your friends

Join an existing game

Enter the room code and the exact player name your group assigned to you. You appear on the map when you join. You can only join before the host starts the match.

Each seat can only be claimed once. Remember your passcode: it lets you log back in from another device or browser, and it cannot be recovered if you forget it.
Core rules

Action Points. Four choices. Last player standing.

MoveSpend 1 AP to move one square up, down, left or right.
AttackSpend 1 AP to hit a player within your range. Three hits eliminate a player.
TransferSpend 1 AP to give 1 AP to another player within your range.
Extend rangeSpend 2 AP to permanently increase your range by one square.

Each player starts with 1 AP, 3 lives and range 2 when they claim their seat, and appears on the map as far as possible from the other players. Seats nobody claims never appear. Range includes horizontal, vertical and diagonal squares (square / Chebyshev distance). Movement is orthogonal only. Players cannot occupy the same square. Eliminated players join the jury: once per day each juror can give 1 AP to a living player, and votes show in the log. Living players receive 1 AP every day at 20:00 Japan time. The database applies this even when everyone is offline.
